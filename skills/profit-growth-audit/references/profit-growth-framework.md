# Profit Growth Framework

The audit should answer one question first:

```text
Which Intelligems tests can grow actual profit, not just conversion rate?
```

## Ranking Criteria

Score each opportunity across:

- Profit impact: GPV, RPV, net revenue, or other available profit-oriented metric.
- Confidence: statistical confidence and sample size.
- Readiness: whether a winner can be implemented without new creative, engineering, or operational work.
- Risk: downside in AOV, margin, shipping cost, refund behavior, or segment concentration.
- Learning value: whether the result creates a reusable testing insight.

## Red Flags

- Conversion rate up, RPV down.
- Conversion rate up, GPV down.
- AOV down enough to offset order-volume gains.
- Strong aggregate result driven by one narrow segment.
- High lift with low visitors or unstable confidence.
- Winner conflicts with brand, margin, inventory, or fulfillment constraints.

## Recommended Audit Table

Use a compact table with:

- Experience name and ID.
- Status.
- Winner or leading variation.
- Primary metric.
- Profit metric.
- Confidence.
- Revenue or profit impact.
- Recommendation.
- Caveat.
